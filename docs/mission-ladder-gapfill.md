## FD-20 Capture The Flag 2 — Test Driver 8

**Infobox:**
- Mode: Factory Driver
- Mission: Checkpoints
- Time: 385 seconds
- Course: Auvergne
- Car: 1994 Porsche 911 Carrera Coupé (993)

**Briefing:**
> "Dieter's away on business tonight and we're going into town to play a little game of 'Capture the Flag'. Do you think you have the juice to beat ole Rolf's time? Last time I drove this route I set a new record and I'm looking to set a newer one tonight. Just keep your eyes open and we'll let you know where to go. Don't drive too crazy, though, because you might get restarted facing the wrong direction."

**Mission Rules:** Checkpoint rally — reach various checkpoints along a route on Auvergne's open roads. Time limit: 385 seconds (6 min 25 sec). Rolf's guidance via radio. Restart facing wrong direction if you crash.

**Course:** Auvergne — French countryside with rolling hills, open roads. Point-to-point checkpoint route.

**Car:** 1994 Porsche 911 Carrera Coupé (993) — the same base 911 used in several earlier missions.

**Route Map:** The infobox image (NFSPU PC Factory Driver 20.jpg) shows a two-page map of Auvergne with a serpentine checkpoint route marked from start to finish. Consecutive numbered flags track progress through the countryside. Golden start orb and golden finish arrow at opposite ends of the route.

## FD-29 Porsche Commercial (Reverse Slide) — Chief Test Driver 7

**Infobox:**
- Mode: Factory Driver
- Mission: Slalom — two 180 degree spins and one 360 degree spin
- Time: 50 seconds
- Course: Weissach Skid Pad
- Car: 1998 Porsche 911 Carrera 3.4 Cabriolet (996)

**Briefing:**
> "We are filming the new Porsche commercial this afternoon. We need you to drive the course. Stephanie usually does it, but she's out of town helping out the racing team. What you have to do is a 180 slide through the cones, drive in reverse to the next set of cones where you have to do a 180 slide to turn yourself around again. After that you'll have to go around the pylons and do a 360 spin in the last set. It's pretty tricky, but I know you can handle it. You'll have to use a manual transmission to make the 180's, but as long as you stay between the pylons and keep your speed up, everything will be fine."
— Dieter, Porsche Executive

**Mission Rules:** Unique reverse-slide mission. Two 180° reverse slides with driving in reverse between them, then a 360° spin. Requires manual transmission. Time limit: 50 seconds.

**Course:** Weissach Skid Pad — specially set up for the commercial filming with pylons and cone gates for the slide manoeuvres.

**Car:** 1998 Porsche 911 Carrera 3.4 Cabriolet (996) — Cabriolet for the commercial (filming needs an open-top car?).

**Route Map:** The infobox image (NFSPU PC Factory Driver 29.jpg) shows the Weissach Skid Pad with a complex route: 180° slide zone at gate 1-2, a lengthy reverse-drive section through gates 2-3, a second 180° slide at gate 3-4, pylon slalom, and a 360° spin zone at the end. Golden start orb and golden finish arrow.

**Screenshot:** `.playwright-mcp/fd-29-porsche-commercial-reverse-slide.png`
