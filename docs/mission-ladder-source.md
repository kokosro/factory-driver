## 1\. Overview & Characters

**Factory Driver** is a game mode where the player is an official Porsche test driver working through company ranks by completing sequential missions. Each mission hands the player a specific car; damage and time limits are common constraints.

**Characters (Teammates):**

- **Rolf** — Chief Test Driver. Gives the player their earliest assignments and final promotion test.
- **Frank** — Senior Test Driver. Friendly rival, sets up bets with Stephanie.
- **Billy** — Junior Test Driver. Brash newcomer who challenges the player repeatedly.
- **Stephanie** — Ace Test Driver (the best). Cools, confident, sets slalom bets with Frank.
- **Dieter** — Porsche Executive. Hands out delivery and executive-urgency missions.
- **Klaus** — Head Mechanic. Gives technical/setup missions (360 spins, snow testing, Boxster mod testing).

**Rank progression:** Applicant → Junior Test Driver → Test Driver → Chief Test Driver → Ace Test Driver.

**Unusual mission types:** Slalom (coned course), Delivery (point-A-to-B with police/traffic), Checkpoints (Capture the Flag — knockout cones in order), Race (multi-driver on open roads with police), Time Trial (circuit), Circuit Race, Reverse Slide.

**Briefing:**
