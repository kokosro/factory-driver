## 2. FIELD PROBES (the throwaway script's method and the pinned numbers)
Method (the script is gone; this is what it did, on the corrected profile of §1.1):
- At each issue (x, z): WorldRoadProfile.describe (road, chainage, signed offset right of travel,
  distance), sample_height raw and corrected, terrain_height, ramp_gradient split into
  along-road and lateral (right-of-travel) components using the loop polyline's tangent.
- Window ±40 m along the loop polyline at 1 m, at offset 0 and at the car's offset: the
  station-to-station grade and the grade change per metre (both maxima and where).
- Step scan ±6 m along at 0.25 m, at offsets −3.75, −3, −2, −1, 0, +1, +2, +3, +3.75 and the
  car's: the largest one-step height jump per offset (a jump per 0.25 m is a discontinuity
  or a grade: 0.025 m per step is a 10 % grade, 0.3 m per step is a wall).
- Range scan: the same, from the flag point forward over the flagged odometer range.
- Cross-section tables through a seam: heights at the nine offsets at rel −30…+30 m,
  relative to the seam's centre height, and which road answered each point.
- Off-loop issues (0006, 0022) use the nearest segment's own centreline for the window.
